How to Choose LED Strip Lights

LED strip lights are thin, flexible strips of small lights with a sticky back. You stick them behind a TV, under a desk or along a bed frame to add soft, colorful light to a room.

See LED strip lights on Amazon

1. Measure first

Strips come in many lengths. Measure where you want them to go, and add a little extra for corners. Check the product page to see if the strip can be cut to size.

2. Pick how you'll control them

  • Remote: simple, no app needed.
  • Phone app: more colors, timers and effects.
  • Voice assistant: check the product page for which assistants it works with.
See app-controlled LED strips on Amazon

3. Prep the surface

Clean and dry the surface before sticking. Dust and grease are the main reasons strips fall down. Mounting clips can help on rough surfaces.

Other things to check

  • Power: plug-in or USB-powered (handy behind a TV).
  • TV backlights: some kits are made to fit common TV sizes.
Tip: Test the strip before you peel the backing, so you know everything works.
