How to Choose Wireless Earbuds

Photo: Pexels

There are hundreds of wireless earbuds out there. The easiest way to choose is to start with how you'll use them — gym, commute, calls or all-day listening — and then check a few key features.

See wireless earbuds on Amazon

Start with how you'll use them

  • Workouts: look for a secure fit (ear hooks or wing tips) and a water/sweat resistance rating.
  • Commuting and travel: active noise cancelling (ANC) helps block out trains, buses and planes.
  • Calls and meetings: check reviews about microphone quality, not just music quality.

Features worth checking

1. Battery life

Brands list battery life for the earbuds alone and with the charging case. Note that noise cancelling usually shortens battery life.

2. Water resistance (IPX rating)

An IPX rating tells you how well they handle sweat and water. A higher number means more protection. Check the product page for the exact rating.

3. Fit and ear tips

Comfort matters most. Look for models that include several sizes of ear tips.

4. Phone compatibility

Some earbuds have extra features that work best with a certain phone brand. Check that the features you want work with your phone.
