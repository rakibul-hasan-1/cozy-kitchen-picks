How to Choose an Air Fryer for a Small Kitchen

An air fryer is a countertop oven that blows hot air around food. It can make foods crispy with little or no oil. The hard part in a small kitchen is picking one that fits — both your counter and your cooking.

See compact air fryers on Amazon

Pick the right size

Air fryers are sold by basket capacity, usually in quarts. As a rough guide:

  • Smaller baskets suit one or two people and snacks.
  • Mid-size baskets suit couples or small families.
  • Large or dual baskets suit families who cook full meals at once.

Measure your counter space and the space under your cabinets before you buy. Check the product's listed dimensions.

Features worth checking

Basket vs. oven style

Basket models are compact and easy to shake. Oven-style models have racks and a door, and some can toast or bake too, but they're usually bigger.

Easy-clean parts

Look for a nonstick basket and check whether parts are listed as dishwasher-safe.

Controls

Digital controls with presets are handy. Simple dial controls are easy to use and have fewer things to break.

Dual baskets

Two baskets let you cook two foods at once. Great for families, but they take more counter space.

Tip: Leave a few inches of space behind and above the air fryer while it runs. Hot air comes out of the vents.

Bottom line

For a small kitchen, buy the smallest size that fits how many people you cook for. Always check the current price, dimensions and customer reviews on the product page before you buy.
