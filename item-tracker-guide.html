How to Choose a Bluetooth Item Tracker

Photo: Pexels

An item tracker is a small tag you attach to things you lose often, like keys, a wallet or a bag. You find it with an app on your phone, and many trackers can play a sound so you can hear where it is.

Most important: does it work with your phone?

Trackers are usually built for one phone system:

  • iPhone users: look for trackers that work with Apple's Find My app.
  • Android users: look for trackers that work with Google's Find Hub (Find My Device) or the tracker brand's own app.

Check the product page carefully before you buy.

Other things to check

Shape

Round tags fit keyrings. Flat, card-shaped trackers slide into a wallet.

Battery

Some use a replaceable coin battery, others recharge. Check the product page for battery type.

Holder or keyring

Some trackers need a separate holder to attach to keys. Check if one is included.

Please use trackers responsibly. They're for finding your own things. Never use one to track a person without their knowledge.
