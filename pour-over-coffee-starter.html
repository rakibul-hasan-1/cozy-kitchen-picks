Pour-Over Coffee Starter Kit: What You Actually Need

Photo: Pexels

Pour-over is a simple way to brew coffee by hand. You pour hot water slowly over ground coffee in a filter. You control every step, so it's easy to adjust the taste. Here is the short list of tools to start.

1. A dripper

The dripper holds the filter and coffee. Cone-shaped and flat-bottom drippers are the two common styles. Both work well for beginners.

See pour-over drippers on Amazon

2. Paper filters

Buy filters made for your dripper's shape and size. Rinse the paper with hot water before brewing to remove any paper taste.

3. A gooseneck kettle

The thin spout lets you pour slowly and evenly. Electric models with temperature control are handy but optional — a stovetop gooseneck works too.

See gooseneck kettles on Amazon

4. A coffee grinder

Freshly ground beans make a big difference. Burr grinders give a more even grind than blade grinders, which helps the coffee taste balanced.

See burr grinders on Amazon

5. A kitchen scale (optional, but helpful)

Weighing coffee and water helps you repeat a cup you liked. A simple digital scale is enough.

See coffee scales on Amazon
Tip: Start by pouring a little water to wet the grounds, wait about 30 seconds, then pour the rest slowly in circles.
