How to Choose a Handheld Milk Frother

A handheld milk frother is a small, battery- or USB-powered whisk. You dip it into milk, press a button, and it spins fast to whip air into the milk. The result is foam you can spoon onto coffee, tea or matcha.

See handheld milk frothers on Amazon

Who it's good for

  • People who like lattes or cappuccinos but don't want a big espresso machine.
  • Small kitchens and dorm rooms with little counter space.
  • Matcha and hot chocolate fans — a frother helps mix powders smoothly.

What to look for

1. Power source

Some frothers use AA batteries, others charge by USB. Battery models are simple; rechargeable models save you from buying batteries. Pick what fits your routine.

2. Speed settings

One speed works fine for most people. Multiple speeds can help if you want thin foam for drinks and thicker foam for toppings.

3. Stand or storage

A stand or wall holder keeps the whisk off the counter and helps it dry. Check if one is included.

4. Easy cleaning

Most handheld frothers clean by spinning them in warm water. Check the product page for whether the whisk head comes off.

Tip: Warm your milk first (not boiling), and keep the whisk just under the surface. That's the easiest way to get nice foam.

Handheld vs. electric frothing pitcher

A handheld frother is small and cheap to store. An electric frothing pitcher heats and froths the milk for you, but takes up more space. If you want one-button convenience, look at pitchers too.
