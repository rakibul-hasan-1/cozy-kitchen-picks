How to Choose an Oil Sprayer

An oil sprayer is a refillable bottle that sprays a thin mist of cooking oil. Instead of pouring oil from the bottle, you spray just a light, even coat on a pan, salad or air fryer basket.

See oil sprayers on Amazon

Who it's good for

  • Air fryer owners — a light spray helps food crisp up.
  • People who want to control how much oil they use.
  • Anyone who wants to use their own oil instead of store-bought spray cans.

What to look for

1. Mist vs. stream

Some sprayers make a fine mist, others a stream, and some do both. A fine mist is best for an even coat.

2. Bottle material

Glass bottles let you see the oil level and are easy to clean. Plastic bottles are lighter and less likely to break.

3. Easy refill and cleaning

A wide opening makes refilling easier. Check if parts are dishwasher-safe.

4. Oil type

Most sprayers work with thin oils like olive or avocado oil. Very thick oils can clog the nozzle, so check the product page.

Tip: Clean the nozzle every so often with warm, soapy water to keep the spray even.
