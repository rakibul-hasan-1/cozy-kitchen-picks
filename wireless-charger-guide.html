How to Choose a Wireless Charging Pad

Photo: Pexels

A wireless charging pad lets you charge your phone by setting it down — no cable to plug in. It's handy on a nightstand or desk. Here's what to check first.

See wireless charging pads on Amazon

1. Does your phone support Qi?

Most wireless chargers use the Qi standard. Check your phone's specs to see if it supports Qi wireless charging. If it doesn't, a pad won't charge it.

2. Check your phone case

Thick cases, metal plates, pop-up grips and cards in a wallet case can block wireless charging. Many chargers list a maximum case thickness on the product page.

3. Expect slower charging

Wireless charging is usually slower than a cable. It's best for overnight charging or topping up at your desk. If speed matters, check the charger's listed wattage and whether your phone supports that speed.

Pad vs. stand

A pad lies flat — great for nightstands. A stand holds your phone upright, so you can see notifications or use it for video calls while it charges.
