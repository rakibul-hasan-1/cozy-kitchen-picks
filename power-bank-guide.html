How to Choose a Portable Power Bank

Photo: Pexels

A power bank is a battery you carry with you to charge your phone, earbuds, tablet or even a laptop. The right one depends on what you charge and how often you're away from an outlet.

See power banks on Amazon

Who it's good for

  • Commuters and students who are out all day.
  • Travelers and people on long flights.
  • Anyone whose phone battery runs low by evening.

What to look for

1. Capacity (mAh)

Capacity is listed in mAh. A bigger number means more charges, but also a heavier bank. Compare the bank's capacity to your phone's battery size to get a rough idea of how many charges you'll get — real-world charges are always a bit less than the math says.

2. Charging speed and ports

Check that the ports match your cables (USB-C, USB-A). If your phone supports fast charging, look for a bank that lists the same fast-charging standard.

3. Laptop charging

Not every power bank can charge a laptop. If you need that, check the product page for USB-C Power Delivery output high enough for your laptop.

4. Size and weight

Slim banks fit in a pocket. Larger ones belong in a bag. Think about where you'll carry it.
