How to Choose an Adjustable Phone Stand

Photo: Pexels

A phone stand holds your phone at a good angle so you don't have to. It's simple, but it makes video calls, watching videos and following recipes much easier.

See adjustable phone stands on Amazon

Ways to use one

  • Hands-free video calls and online meetings.
  • Following recipes in the kitchen without touching the screen with messy hands.
  • Watching videos at your desk or on a table.

What to look for

1. Fits your phone and case

Check the supported phone sizes and the opening width, with your case on.

2. Adjustable angle and height

Stands with an adjustable arm let you raise the phone to eye level, which is better for video calls.

3. Stable base

A heavier base or non-slip pads keep the phone from tipping when you tap the screen.

4. Charging access

Look for a gap at the bottom so you can charge while the phone sits in the stand.

5. Foldable

Foldable stands are easy to pack in a bag for travel.

Tip: For video calls, set the camera at eye level. It looks more natural than pointing up from the desk.
