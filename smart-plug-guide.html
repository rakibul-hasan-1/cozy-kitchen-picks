Smart Plugs for Beginners

A smart plug goes between your wall outlet and a device, like a lamp or fan. It lets you turn that device on and off from your phone, set schedules, or use voice commands. It's one of the easiest ways to start a smart home.

See smart plugs on Amazon

Easy ways to use one

  • Turn on a lamp automatically when it gets dark.
  • Schedule a fan to turn on before you get home.
  • Turn off holiday lights or chargers at a set time.
  • Make it look like someone is home while you travel.

What to check before you buy

1. Voice assistant support

Check that the plug works with the assistant you use, such as Alexa, Google Assistant or Apple Home. Look for the "works with" labels on the product page.

2. Wi-Fi band

Many smart plugs only connect to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Most home routers have this, but check your router if setup fails.

3. Size

Some plugs are bulky and block the second outlet. Compact or "mini" designs leave room for another plug.

4. Energy monitoring

Some models show how much power a device uses. It's handy if you want to track energy, but not needed for basic on/off use.

5. Outdoor use

Use only plugs rated for outdoor use outside, such as for patio or holiday lights.

Safety tip: Check the plug's maximum load on the product page, and don't use it with high-power devices beyond that limit. Never leave heating devices running unattended.
